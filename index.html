Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Transparent answers to common questions about our private investigation process, legal standards, and fees.

How confidential are your investigation services?

We maintain absolute confidentiality. Your identity, contact information, and case details are never disclosed to third parties under any circumstances. All intelligence is encrypted, and records are securely handed over or permanently purged upon final briefing.

What is the cost of an investigation?

Costs vary according to case complexity, operational duration, logistics, and resource allocation. Following your private consultation, we provide a transparent, fixed fee structure with no hidden charges.

Do I have to pay the full amount upfront?

No. We only require a reasonable mobilization retainer to commence fieldwork. The remaining balance is payable upon assignment completion and formal delivery of verified reports and evidence.

Is hiring a private detective agency legal in India?

Yes. Sanskari Detective Agency operates strictly within Indian legal frameworks, conforming to established constitutional protections and ethical investigative practices.

How long does an investigation take?

Routine assignments like pre-matrimonial screenings typically conclude within 3 to 7 days, whereas complex missing person searches or corporate forensic audits may require 1 to 3 weeks. An estimated timeline is provided at initial consultation.

Which cities do you have offices in and operate?

Our Headquarters is based in Patna, Bihar, supported by regional branches in Begusarai and Muzaffarpur. Furthermore, our nationwide investigative network extends to Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, and key metros across India.

How do I start a consultation secretly?

You can reach us directly at +91 6299579120 or +91 8210646763, or send a message via our encrypted WhatsApp desk. We will schedule a private, discreet briefing without disclosing your identity.
